How Droboard Contracts Work
1
Droboard offers you a non-exclusive contract for qualifying stories. You keep full rights — you can still publish elsewhere.
2
Once signed, your story joins the revenue programme. You earn 50% of all platform profit your story generates.
3
Signing Bonus is released only when your story hits 150,000 words AND the story has earned a total of $40 platform profit (your share = $20).
💡 Word count and earnings are tracked in real time. You'll see live progress bars on each contract below.
